## Caveats

1. **The stem "miracl" does not actually catch "miraculous."** That word is spelled m-i-r-a-c-**u**-l-o-u-s, so it has no "miracl" substring. To catch miracle, miracles, *and* miraculous the stem must be **"mirac"**. I counted both ways; for these three talks the paragraph totals are identical either way, so the answer above is unaffected — but the pattern as specified would silently miss "miraculous" in other talks.

2. **"Paragraph" = one `<p>` element** in the talk body, excluding empty ones. Headings, pull-quote callouts, the speaker bio, and the footnotes block are outside the body and were not counted. Counting a paragraph once regardless of how many times the word appears in it.

3. Counts are paragraphs, not word occurrences. Wilcox has 2 paragraphs but 4 word occurrences.
