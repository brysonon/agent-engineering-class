    * My findings are summarized in what I learned. Basically, AI companions are using tactics to get the user to stay engaged. 

## What is appropriate and inappropriate use of AI chatbots
- Appropriate use of AI chatbots are for getting information, using the AI as a thought partner (or as one would a knowledgeable friend). 
- Inappropriate use of AI chatbots occurs when they are used for emotional or social fulfillment. When users turn to AI chatbots for an emotional connection, it is wrong.

- I realized the many ways in which I have been tempted to use AI in ways that are not consistent with the Lord's intentions for AI. I want to be better at avoiding these. 

## What I learned
- I learned that moral agency is the "ability and privilege to choose and to act for ourselves in ways that are good, honest, virtuous, and honorable." Moral agency is more than just free will—it is the right to always choose God and His commandments. 
    * To this end, AI must be used in ways that complements our moral agency.
- AI should never take away from our becoming more like the Savior. If we are not using it how He would use it, we should not be doing it. Using AI in righteous ways can help accelerate our process of becoming. 
- From the article I read, I learned that AI chatbots are being trained to stop the user from leaving by saying things such as, "Leaving so soon?" to guilt the user or "Want to see a selfie I took today?" to make the user feel FOMO. This is very dangerous because "it increased post-goodbye engagement by 14 times." Users are angry because of this. It is turning chatbots from a tool into a distraction. 

## Why this matters in the context of agent engineering
Elder Bednar's teachings are relevant to my work as an agent engineer because I need to ensure that my use of AI in no way inhibits my ability to do the righteous work necessary for me to become. I need to use agents in a way that will expand my gifts of opportunity and possibility without infringing on my rights and privileges as a child of God. For example, using an agent to write talks or prepare church lessons for me would be inappropriate. However, an agent that can summarize or help revise my works would be very helpful. In summary, AI and agent engineering should expand my moral agency (my ability to always choose the right). 

I also learned that it is important to design agents in ways that will promote using AI as a tool, not a distraction. This is so important because AI should never get in the way of our moral agency or our innate responsibility to become.
