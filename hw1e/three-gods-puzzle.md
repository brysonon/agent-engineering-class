The Puzzle

The Setup: 
Three gods—A, B, and C—are named True, False, and Random in no particular order.True always tells the truth.False always lies.Random chooses randomly to tell the truth or lie.

The Goal: Figure out the identities of A, B, and C by asking them three yes/no questions.

The Rules:Each question must be directed to exactly one god.The gods understand English, but will answer in their own language using only "da" and "ja". You do not know which word means "yes" and which means "no".You can ask a god a question whose answer depends on the answer to a previous question.The random god's coin flip decides whether he tells the truth or lies for each separate question.
